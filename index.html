What is the Five Elements (五行) name scoring? 什么是五行姓名评分？

The Five Elements (Wu Xing / 五行) system is a fundamental concept in Chinese philosophy, consisting of Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), and Water (水). In Chinese naming tradition, each character's stroke count maps to one of these elements. A well-balanced name should harmonize these elements, creating a favorable energy pattern. Our scoring tool analyzes this balance along with other traditional factors.

五行（金木水火土）是中国哲学的基本概念。在中国取名传统中，每个字的笔画数对应五行之一。一个好名字应该使这些元素和谐，创造有利的能量格局。我们的评分工具会分析这种平衡以及其他传统因素。

For English names, we trace origins to Latin, Greek, Hebrew, Germanic, and other linguistic roots. For Chinese names, we analyze individual character meanings and their combinations. While no database is exhaustive, we strive for accuracy and regularly update our data.

我们的姓名含义数据库涵盖数千个常见英文和中文名字的已验证词源数据。对于英文名字，我们追溯到拉丁语、希腊语、希伯来语、日耳曼语等语言根源。对于中文名字，我们分析单个字的含义及其组合。虽然没有数据库是完美的，但我们努力保持准确性并定期更新数据。
